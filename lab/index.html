How it works

  1. Claude tab: my PON, cable, poles, 1:8s and drops. Tap Pin, then tap a spot to say what's wrong and which rule it breaks.
  2. Yours tab: the same homes and erven. Place the 1:16, poles, cable, 1:8s and drops yourself.
  3. Rules tab: mark each of my rules right or wrong, fix the wording, add your own.
  4. Tap Send: it gives you a picture and a text block. Paste both into Relay.
